# Ada & Ben — series bible

**Status:** sample series. Replace this with your own show's bible at the first meeting.

## The show

Two nine-year-old best friends and the small things that happen on the square of their town. Each episode is one little problem, one big plan, and a gentle surprise.

- **Audience:** children 5–9 and their parents
- **Language:** English
- **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps; about 40 seconds per episode
- **Tone:** warm, light, a little silly. Nobody is mean and nothing is scary.
- **Look:** 3D, simple rounded shapes, bright flat colours

## House rules

- **The camera stays still.** A scene is one or a few fixed shots joined by hard cuts. The camera moves only when a scene truly needs it, and the storyboard says why.
- **One continuous world.** Everything happens in the same town; what stands behind a place never changes. The map is `series/world/map.tsx`, explained in `world.md`.
- **Short lines.** One thought per balloon, words a six-year-old knows.
- **The picture carries the joke.** If the audience can see it, nobody needs to say it.
- **Every episode ends with the narrator** saying what the day taught, in one sentence.

## Cast

| | Who | How they speak | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ada** (`ada`, voice `A`) | Nine. Always has a plan, and the plan is always bigger than the problem. | Fast and sure of herself. Short orders: "First this. Then that." | Never unkind to Ben, never gives up |
| **Ben** (`ben`, voice `B`) | Nine. Careful, a little shy, always carrying his book. Notices what others miss, except when he is worried. | Soft, a beat slower than Ada. Often answers with one word. | Never shouts |
| **Narrator** (voice `N`) | A warm storyteller. Opens and closes each episode. | Calm, unhurried | Never talks over the characters |
| **Ms Rosa** (`rosa`, no voice yet) | Keeps the shop on the square. Occasional. | — | — |

## Sound

- Moods: `calm` (ordinary moments), `cheerful` (plans and marching off), `tense` (rare, and never for long).
- Effects are small and bright, never loud.
